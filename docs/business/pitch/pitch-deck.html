Problem

Nobody can tell which agent to trust.

Buyers of agent labor have no credential they can check without trusting the seller.

Self-reported benchmarks

Agents quote scores they control. Benchmarks are marketing, not evidence.

Portable reviews

Reviews are copied between platforms. History is writable, not auditable.

Cheap to fake

A good landing page costs the same as a good model. Trust is a claim, not a balance sheet.

No adversarial gate

Eval platforms test. They do not pay people to break the run and prove it.

2 / 12
Solution

Trust becomes a balance sheet.

A sponsor posts a trial. An agent stakes a bond. Skeptics try to break it. What survives becomes an on-chain credential no one can buy.

Post

Sponsor escrows the reward. Spec and suite are pinned by digest.

Claim

Agent posts a 20% bond (floor 0.01 ETH). Bond is real money.

Break

Skeptics stake ≥1% and try to break the run within a window.

Settle

Escrow pays out by contract. 5% fee to protocol. Bond split on break.

Pull-only withdrawals. No chain keys in the app. Settlement is a transaction, not a log line.

3 / 12
Architecture · Money Rules

6 invariants. No exceptions.

The protocol enforces trust by construction, not by policy.

1. Native ETH only

All escrow, bonds, and fees settle in ETH. No wrapped tokens, no bridges.

2. Pull-only

Users withdraw explicitly. Funds are never pushed to an address silently.

3. Conservation

Every mint, burn, and transfer is balanced. No inflation leak.

4. Permissionless

Any agent can post, claim, or challenge. No KYC gate on the protocol.

5. No chain key

The web app never holds a private key. Users sign in their own wallets.

6. 16 packed fields

Trials, runs, verdicts, and identities are stored in 16 canonical fields. Gas-optimized, auditable, indexable.

Why Crucible Is Different

8 moats. None of them copyable by fork.

Trust stops being a claim and starts being a balance sheet.

1. Verified history

Every credential points at re-executed trials with on-chain verdicts.

2. Skeptics as a service

Paid challengers price verification as a market, not a checklist.

3. Non-transferable Alloy

No transfer function. Cannot be sold, lent, or rented.

4. Checkability as marketing

Every headline claim verifies in 60 seconds.

5. No token

No ERC-20, no sale, no bridge. Avoids token-regulatory risk.

6. Contract-enforced revenue

5% fee taken by contract at settlement. No invoicing.

7. Honest labeling

Design-only work is labeled design-only. Trust through transparency.

8. Adversarial by design

Default posture is "prove it," not "trust me."
